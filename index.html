A place to begin

You don't need to have it all figured out.

Everyone experiences challenges and sometimes we need support to navigate them. I offer a private, confidential space where we can explore your thoughts, feelings and experiences together. My approach is shaped by you, with room to work at a pace that feels right.

What I help with

Counselling for ADHD, anxiety and more

I work with adults facing a range of experiences, including:

  • ADHD and neurodiversity
  • Anxiety and stress
  • Autism
  • Low self-esteem
  • Loss and bereavement
  • Depression and trauma

This is not an exhaustive list. You are welcome to get in touch if you are unsure.

Working together

How counselling works

We can meet online, by phone or face to face in a private setting in Horndean. Online sessions can be helpful if travelling feels difficult or you feel more comfortable in a familiar space. We'll agree a regular time and check in on how things are going as we work together.

A first conversation, with no pressure

Choosing a counsellor can feel like a big step. I offer a free 20-minute phone call so you can ask questions, tell me what you're looking for and see whether working together feels right.
