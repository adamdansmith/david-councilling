Counselling & Psychotherapy

What I can help with

I work with adults aged 18 and over on:

  • ADHD and neurodiversity
  • Anxiety and stress
  • Autism
  • Low self-esteem
  • Bereavement and loss
  • Depression and trauma
  • Chronic fatigue / ME
  • Health-related concerns

My practice

I run a private practice both online and in person in Horndean, Hampshire, and I also work for I’m All Ears counselling service in Surrey. I am a registered member of the British Association for Counselling and Psychotherapy (BACP) and follow its ethical framework for good practice. Face-to-face sessions take place in a private, confidential home setting. There is on-street parking and a bus stop about two minutes’ walk away.

How I work

If you are considering counselling, you are taking the first step towards addressing a problem or discomfort in your life. Something may not be working for you, or you may feel unsure or distressed without knowing why. Everyone experiences challenges, and sometimes we need additional support to navigate them. As a trained psychotherapist, I offer a professional approach to self-exploration and discovery within the therapeutic relationship. Together, we can explore your thoughts, feelings and behaviours to support healing and growth.

Your first session

Before our first session, I offer a free 20-minute phone call to discuss what you need and answer any questions. If you decide to continue, we will arrange weekly 50-minute sessions at a regular time and review our progress together. Sessions are available online, by phone or face to face.
