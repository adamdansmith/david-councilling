Counselling in Horndean and online

Welcome, I am David Billington. I am available online, and face to face in Horndean, Hampshire. I provide a safe place where you can confidentially share your life, your story and what may be painful and confusing for you. Together we can share your burden and find a new way of being. You can make incredible changes and empower yourself to take the right path for you.

About my practice

I am a registered member of the British Association for Counselling and Psychotherapy (BACP) and follow its ethical framework. Alongside my private practice, I work for I’m All Ears counselling service in Surrey. I offer a confidential space to explore what is happening in your life and what you would like to change.

Counselling for ADHD, anxiety and more

I work with adults facing a range of experiences, including:

  • ADHD and neurodiversity
  • Anxiety and stress
  • Autism
  • Low self-esteem
  • Loss and bereavement
  • Depression and trauma

This is not an exhaustive list. You are welcome to get in touch if you are unsure.

How I work

Everyone experiences challenges, and sometimes we need support to navigate them. Counselling gives us time to explore and understand your thoughts, feelings and behaviours together. Sessions are available face to face in Horndean, online or by phone. We can work at a pace that suits you and review what is helpful as we go.

A first conversation, with no pressure

Choosing a counsellor can feel like a big step. I offer a free 20-minute phone call so you can ask questions, tell me what you're looking for and see whether working together feels right.
